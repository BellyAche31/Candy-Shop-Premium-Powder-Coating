Our Process

Every mag goes through the same thorough prep before it ever sees color.

1. Strip to Metal

All old paint, clear coat, and coating is fully stripped down to bare metal.

2. Chemical Treatment

The bare metal is chemically treated to remove contaminants and help the powder coat bond properly.

3. Wet Blasted

Wheels are wet blasted for a clean, uniform surface ready for your chosen color.
